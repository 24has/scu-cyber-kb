## Lockie's top 5 tips

<div class="lockie-tips">

<div class="lockie-tip">

**1. Pause before you click**

Be cautious of unexpected emails, links, attachments, texts, and requests for personal information. If something feels off, stop and check before acting.

</div>

<div class="lockie-tip">

**2. Protect your accounts**

Use a strong passphrase and multi-factor authentication (MFA). It makes it much harder for attackers to access your accounts.

</div>

<div class="lockie-tip">

**3. Report suspicious emails**

Not sure if an email is legitimate? Use the **Report Phishing** button in Outlook. If you spot something suspicious, report it.

</div>

<div class="lockie-tip">

**4. Keep your devices updated**

Install updates promptly to help protect against the latest security threats. Turn on automatic updates when you can.

</div>

<div class="lockie-tip">

**5. Handle information securely**

Only access, share, and store university information in approved locations. Use OneDrive for SCU work — do not save sensitive data to personal devices or cloud accounts.

</div>

</div>

<div class="callout">

**Cyber resilience starts with you.** Stay aware. Stay secure. Stay resilient.

</div>

## Where you will see Lockie

Lockie appears across SCU cyber awareness materials — on posters, in emails, on digital signage, and here on the cyber resilience site. Each time you see Lockie, there is a tip or reminder worth reading.

New tips and materials are added regularly. Check back or follow the Technology Services communications for updates.
