## How to print

Each poster is designed at A4. Print in colour for best results. Right-click the poster preview and save as image, or take a screenshot.

For A3 printing, scale up in your printer settings.

More posters will be added as new topics come up. If you have a topic you would like to see, let Technology Services know.
