## What you can do

- Use SCU systems for work, study, and reasonable personal use
- Access your files and applications from any device
- Connect personal devices to the campus Wi-Fi
- Use approved cloud services (OneDrive, Blackboard, etc.)

## What you cannot do

- Share your SCU account credentials with anyone
- Use SCU systems for commercial purposes without approval
- Access or attempt to access systems or data you are not authorised to use
- Install unauthorised software on university devices
- Use SCU systems to harass, bully, or harm others
- Connect unauthorised network devices to the SCU network

## Monitoring

SCU monitors network traffic and system logs to protect the University. This is done in accordance with the Privacy Act and the Acceptable Use of ICT Resources Policy.

## Breaches

Breaches of this policy may result in loss of access, disciplinary action, or referral to law enforcement.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, contact Technology Services before doing it.
