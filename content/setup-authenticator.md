**Requirements:** a smartphone (iPhone or Android) with internet access.

<div class="steps">

1. Install the **Microsoft Authenticator** app from the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android). Do not open the app yet.
2. On your computer, go to [mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info) and sign in.
3. Click **Add sign-in method**, select **Authenticator app**, then click **Add**.
4. A QR code appears on your computer. Keep this page open.
5. Open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone. Tap the plus icon (+) or **Add account**, select **Work or school account**, then **Scan QR code**. Point your phone camera at the QR code.
6. A test notification appears on your phone. Open it and tap **Approve**. Enter the two-digit number from your computer screen into the app.
7. Click **Next** then **Done** on your computer. Confirm Microsoft Authenticator appears under your sign-in methods.

</div>

<div class="callout callout--gold">

If you do not receive the notification, open the app and tap your SCU account to approve manually.

</div>

## Signing in with Microsoft Authenticator

<div class="steps">

1. Sign in with your SCU email and password.
2. A notification appears on your phone. Open it.
3. Enter the two-digit number shown on your computer screen into the app.
4. Tap **Yes** or **Approve**.

</div>
