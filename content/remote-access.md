## Accessing SCU systems from off-campus

Some SCU systems (shared drives, internal tools, certain applications) are only accessible from the campus network. To access them from home or elsewhere, use the SCU VPN.

## Setting up the VPN

1. Download the FortiClient VPN app from the SCU software centre or your device app store.
2. Enter the VPN server address: `vpn.scu.edu.au`
3. Sign in with your SCU credentials.
4. You are now on the SCU network.

## What the VPN does

The VPN creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and the SCU network. It protects your traffic on public Wi-Fi and lets you access systems that are not exposed to the internet.

## When to use it

- Accessing shared drives (H: drive, department shares)
- Using internal applications
- Working with sensitive university data on public networks

You do not need the VPN for email, OneDrive, Blackboard, or other cloud services.

## Problems?

If you cannot connect, contact the Technology Services Service Desk.
