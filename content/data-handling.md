## Classifying data

University information falls into three categories:

- **Public** — anyone can access it (website content, course catalogues)
- **Internal** — for staff and students only (internal memos, meeting notes)
- **Restricted** — confidential or sensitive (student records, financial data, research data)

## Where to store it

- **OneDrive** — for all internal and restricted data. Included with your SCU account.
- **Shared drives** — for department files. Access-controlled by group.
- **Never** — personal cloud accounts (Google Drive, Dropbox), personal email, or unencrypted USB drives.

## Sharing data

- Use OneDrive links instead of email attachments.
- Set link permissions to "People with existing access" or "Specific people" for sensitive files.
- Do not share Restricted data with external parties without approval.

## Sending data externally

If you must send Restricted data outside SCU, use the approved secure file transfer method or encrypt the file. Contact Technology Services if you are unsure.

## Disposal

Delete files securely when they are no longer needed. For Restricted data, use the secure deletion method or contact Technology Services for guidance.
