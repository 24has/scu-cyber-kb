**Before you start:** have your SCU username, password, and current MFA method (SMS or voice call) ready. If using a phone, turn Bluetooth on and keep your phone nearby.

<div class="steps">

1. On your computer, open a browser and go to [mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info](https://mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info)
2. Sign in with your SCU email and password. Complete MFA using your current method if asked.
3. Click **Add sign-in method**, select **Passkey** from the dropdown, then click **Add**.
4. Choose where to store your passkey:
   - **This device:** the passkey saves to your computer. Follow the prompts to use Windows Hello, Touch ID, or your PIN.
   - **Phone or tablet:** a QR code appears on your screen. Scan it with your phone camera and authenticate with your fingerprint, face, or PIN. From then on, your phone will confirm sign-ins from this computer.
   - **Security key:** insert your USB key or tap your NFC key. Touch the key's button or sensor when prompted.
5. After the dialog closes, confirm **Passkey** appears under your sign-in methods on the Security info page.

</div>

<div class="callout callout--gold">

If you registered your passkey on your phone, your phone will show a prompt asking you to verify when signing in. Keep Bluetooth on for both devices.

</div>

## Signing in with a passkey

<div class="steps">

1. Sign in with your SCU email and password as usual.
2. When the MFA prompt appears, your browser will offer to use your passkey. Click **Continue** or **Use passkey**.
3. Authenticate using your fingerprint, face, device PIN, or security key.
4. Done. No codes, no waiting.

</div>
